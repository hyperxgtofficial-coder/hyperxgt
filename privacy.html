Privacy Policy

How HyperXGT collects, protects, uses, and safeguards customer data across our website and service platform.

HyperXGT ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and secure user data when you visit hyperxgt.com or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to process your store orders and provide customer support:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, shipping address, pincode, contact phone number, and email address.
  • Account Details: Login credentials, saved shipping addresses, order history, and Garage preferences.
  • Payment Tokens: Encrypted payment transaction IDs processed securely through PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Cashfree / Razorpay). We never store raw credit card numbers or banking passwords.
  • Uploaded Customer Media: Reviews, testimonials, unboxing images, and unboxing videos voluntarily submitted for discount coupons or community features.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly utilized to:

  • Process, fulfill, and dispatch your RC product orders via our courier network (Shiprocket / Bluedart / Delhivery).
  • Dispatch automated order confirmations, tax invoices, and shipping tracking updates via email (Resend API) and WhatsApp.
  • Process customer review submissions and issue 10% discount codes upon validation.
  • Prevent fraud and maintain platform security.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We only share necessary data with trusted technology partners who assist in operating our e-commerce infrastructure:

  • Logistics Partners: Shiprocket API for generating shipping labels and tracking AWBs.
  • Payment Gateways: Cashfree Payments & Razorpay for encrypted checkout transactions.
  • Database & Auth Hosting: Supabase PostgreSQL Database & Vercel Global Serverless Infrastructure.
  • Email Service: Resend API for transactional email communication.

4. Data Security & Cookies

All data transmission across hyperxgt.com is encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology. We use browser Local Storage and minimal session cookies to persist your active cart items and customer account login state.
